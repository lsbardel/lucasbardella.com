# Information for Recruiters

*Last updated: August 2026, London, UK. A 2 minute read.*

Thank you for reaching out. This page summarises my current situation and the terms under which I engage with clients, so we can quickly establish whether an opportunity is a good fit.

## Current Engagement

For what I am currently working on, see my [now page](/now).

## How I Work

I engage **exclusively as a consultant through [Quantmind](https://quantmind.com)**, contracted business to business.

I am not considering employed positions at this time.

## Preferred Clients

I work best with **scale-ups and established firms**: organisations with secured funding, a proven product, and a clear mandate for the work they need done.

For startups, please provide runway and founders information upfront, as due diligence will be carried out before any engagement.

## Remuneration

For a commitment of five days per week the expected remuneration will be provided on request.
Part-time arrangements can be discussed on a pro-rata basis.

Bonus or performance-related remuneration is taken into consideration, depending on the company.

Equity is not something I consider as part of an initial engagement. Compensation should be entirely in cash, although equity can become part of the conversation once a working relationship is established.

## Location and Travel

- I am **based in London** and work **remotely**.
- For clients outside London, I am happy to travel when required, for up to one week every two months.
- For London-based engagements, I can be in the office **at most once or twice per week**.
- I am **not considering relocation**.

## Get in Touch

If your opportunity fits within these terms, I would be glad to hear about it. I prefer an initial approach by **email** rather than a call, so please write to [luca@quantmind.com](mailto:luca@quantmind.com) with the details. You can also find me on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/lucasbardella) or see my full background on the [CV page](/cv).
