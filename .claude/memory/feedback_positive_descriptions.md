---
name: feedback-positive-descriptions
description: Page and post descriptions state plainly what something is or does, never negatives or what other people think
metadata:
  type: feedback
---

Frontmatter `description` fields (also shown as the hero subtitle) must say in a nutshell what the subject is or does, in positive terms. No "not X", no "most people think", no framing against a misconception.

**Why:** the user rejected "Most people think I am a software engineer..." and then "I am an algorithmic trader, not a software engineer..." for the "What I Actually Do" post: who cares what others think, and negatives do not belong in a description.

**How to apply:** when writing or editing any description, lead with what it is, then what it does. Contrast and misconceptions can live in the body, not the description. Related: [[feedback-no-dashes]].
